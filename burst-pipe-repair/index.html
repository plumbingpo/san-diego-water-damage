Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. A single burst pipe can release 4 to 8 gallons of water per minute, which is 240 to 480 gallons per hour flooding through your walls, ceilings and floors. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

Day or night, San Diego Restoration Co responds to burst pipe emergencies across San Diego and the nearby communities. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. Hidden moisture leads to mold and a deteriorating structure, so we use moisture detection equipment and thermal imaging to map the water's path and make sure all affected areas get dried.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze

Why Professional Burst Pipe Repair Matters

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Our technicians find all the wet areas at the start by tracing migration with thermal imaging and meter readings.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

How to Tell You Need Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Damp, moldy odors around sinks, tubs or walls that hide pipes
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • Exposed pipes that drip, mist or shoot water
  • Water pressure that falls off abruptly at every fixture
