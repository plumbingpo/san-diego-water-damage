How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in San Diego

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
